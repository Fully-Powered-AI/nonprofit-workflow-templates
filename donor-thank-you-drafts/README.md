# Donor thank-you drafts

Donor services staff get one draft thank-you for each new gift, written from the confirmed gift details, the donor's communication preferences and the organization's approved program language. A person checks and sends every message; nothing asks for another gift.

*Workflow template · Nonprofits · Marketing and content · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflow templates/donor-thank-you-drafts/)*

## The outcome

Every confirmed gift has a warm, specific thank-you waiting for a person to read: the donor's preferred name, the gift as the register records it, the line of program language your organization has already approved (a tribute gift gets the approved tribute line and then the fund line), in the channel the donor asked for. The team spends its time on the personal touches that matter instead of assembling the same message from five places.

Gifts that need a person's judgment are never papered over. Anonymous gifts, disputed or unconfirmed amounts and donors who asked not to be contacted are set aside with the role that decides. A question about tax treatment or the value of donated goods is drafted around: the thanks goes out without touching the question, and the draft is marked to send to finance. The draft says nothing about a tax receipt and never asks for anything. Donor services decides what goes out.

## Who starts the work, and when

Someone in donor services starts it after a batch of gifts has been confirmed in the gift register, usually the same day the gifts are posted or at the end of the week. Donor services reads each draft, changes it as they see fit and sends it. Finance is consulted only where the batch raises a question about receipts, values or disputed amounts.

## What they supply

- The gift register export for the batch: gift number, date, donor, amount, type of gift, fund, whether the gift is anonymous, its status and any notes.
- Each donor's communication preferences: preferred name, channel, whether the amount may be named, and any do-not-contact instruction.
- The approved program language: short lines your organization has already signed off, each tied to a fund, with the date it was last reviewed.
- Your settings: who signs, the currency, how many days a thank-you should take, and how long each message may be.

## What comes out

A batch page: one draft for each gift that can be thanked, each with its channel and the approved line it used, then a list of gifts set aside with the reason and who decides, then a check that every row in the register is accounted for. Where something is missing or doubtful, the draft carries a plain note for the person sending it.

## What has to change for your company

Decide who signs and in what voice. Write your approved program lines, one per fund, with an owner and a review date. Set how soon a thank-you should go out and how long it may be. Decide whether a gift amount is named by default or only when the donor has said so. Agree who handles anonymous gifts, disputed amounts and tax or valuation questions, and whether finance supplies a standard receipt line for the template to place unchanged. Keep the receipt itself in your giving system, where it already lives.
