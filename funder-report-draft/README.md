# Funder report draft

Before a funder report is due, the grants manager gets a draft written to the funder's own questions, with every number tied to a record, an index of the evidence behind each statement and a list of what is still unanswered. People verify it, approve it and submit it.

*Workflow template · Nonprofits · Finance and accounting · version 0.1.0 · [Workflows Directory](https://fullypowered.ai/workflows/funder-report-draft/)*

## The outcome

A funder report is mostly gathering. The questions are the funder's, the records sit in four places, and the draft is written in a rush in the last week. This workflow template does the gathering and the first draft in one pass, early enough that the gaps can still be filled.

You receive the report written question by question to the funder's template, each statement tied to the record behind it. Activities are reported as activities: sessions held, people attending, training days. A result appears only where an outcome measure your program lead approved has a recorded figure. Spending comes only from lines your finance lead has reviewed. Where the records cannot answer a question, the draft says so and lists what is needed, from whom.

The program lead still verifies what was delivered, the finance lead still confirms the spending, and the grants manager still approves the text and submits it.

## Who starts the work, and when

The grants manager starts it as a reporting date approaches, once the period's activity records are in and finance has reviewed the spending. It can run again as missing records arrive. The program lead and the finance lead answer the questions addressed to them.

## What they supply

- **The funder's reporting template,** with its questions, limits and any number it asks for.
- **The reporting period, the due date and the currency of the amounts.**
- **Activity records** for the period, each with the record that backs it.
- **Approved outcome measures,** with targets and the figures recorded so far.
- **Finance-reviewed spending** by budget line.
- **The program lead's notes** on changes and difficulties, what was planned for the period, and the previous report for the wording of earlier commitments, if you have them.

## What comes out

One draft in a fixed order: the narrative by funder question with each question marked drafted, drafted in part or not answered; the workings for every number; the records that were set aside and why; an evidence index; and a numbered list of unanswered questions, each for one named role. It ends with the three approvals needed before anything is sent.

It reads nothing into the award. A question about what a condition means, or whether a change is allowed, goes to the grants manager as a question and is not answered.

## What has to change for your company

Keep the funder's template for each grant and its limits. Check that your exports use the column headers the workflow template expects, or change them in both places. Decide who is the program lead, the finance lead and the grants manager in your team, and whether one person can hold two of those roles. Agree which records count as evidence for each kind of statement, and how measures get approved. Adjust the wording rules to your organization's voice. It runs in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, from files and notes you supply; it needs no connection to your grants or accounting system.

## Start using it

1. Read [EXAMPLE.md](EXAMPLE.md) to see what a good result looks like.
2. Open [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and set every item under "What to customize" to your own rules, roles and limits.
3. Give your AI assistant the instruction block and the output format from the template, with your own limits in place of the ones the instructions leave open in square brackets. Paste them in as custom instructions in ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini, or copy this whole folder into a product that loads `SKILL.md` packages. This folder stands alone; you do not need the rest of the repository.
4. Run it on past work first, using the reviewer checklist and acceptance tests in the template, before anyone relies on it.
