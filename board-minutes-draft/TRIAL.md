# Board minutes draft: trial on the invented example

- **Product:** Claude, run in Claude Code
- **Model:** Claude Sonnet 5.5 (model ID `claude-sonnet-5-5`)
- **Date:** 2026-11-10

The inputs are the invented example in `public/example.md` (Harrow Meadow Reading Trust, the board meeting of 3 November 2026). No client data was used.

This is the second run. A first run on the same day, by a reader who had not written the template, found that the example output did not follow the output format, restated some inputs in other words, and that the pasted instruction block left out procedure rules, and it listed eight places where the instructions were ambiguous. The template was then revised: the pasted block now carries the blank-time, off-roster, unlisted-document, closed-session, unmatched-notes and decline rules; the format has a closed-session line; and the template now says how to print a due condition, how a match on content counts, the order of action numbers, one gap per action, and whether attendance facts are remarks. The first run's record is replaced by this one. This run was made by the same session that revised the template, so it is a weaker test than a blind reader: I read the revised pasted block and the six inputs, produced the draft from them, and compared it with the example afterwards. The example's output was then rebuilt to match this run.

## Inputs used

All six inputs listed under "The inputs" in `public/example.md`, unchanged from the first run. No sample of earlier minutes was supplied.

## What was observed

**Every required part was produced.** Meeting block, attendance, a proceedings entry for each of the 8 agenda items, the 3 motions, the document list, the actions, the blank approval lines, the gaps and the check. The counts agree: 8 items, 3 motions, 4 documents, 4 actions, 5 gaps. The voting members present at each vote counted 6, 6 and 5, and each equalled for + against + abstain + recused. No instruction told me to stop: the headers matched, no number repeated, and the notes and the vote record agreed on every mover, seconder and tally.

**The rules held.** I wrote no quorum statement and no opinion on whether any vote was valid. Motion 3's blank result is printed as "[RESULT NOT RECORDED: G3]" and I did not say it passed or failed. The volunteer handbook suggestion, which has no motion row, is marked as noted and not as a motion, with no motion text invented. The action with no owner says "Owner not stated". Actions A1, A2 and A4 use the notes' own words for owner, action and due date, including "by 10 Nov" and the condition "when the landlord's copy arrives". The closed session has no content. The D2 document and item 3 carry gap G1.

**Choices the revised instructions settled.** A due date that is a condition is printed as given with no gap (A2). The last line of the notes about the insurance policy matches item 4 by content and was placed there, with the placement stated in G2. Actions are numbered in the order of the draft. The owner-less, date-less action has one gap (G2). The abstainer and the director who left are attendance facts: the departure of Jessamyn Orr and the arrival of Lowell Teague are named, as the roster has them, and the abstention is counted and not named, because the format's Votes line has room only for recused names.

**Where I still had to choose.**

1. *"Out" time of the closed session.* The notes give the start (19:15) and the time two people returned (19:22), not the end. I printed "[not stated: G5]" and did not use 19:22 or the 19:25 adjournment as the end. The instruction block says to mark a missing time; it does not say that 19:25 may not stand in for it.
2. *Which statement the board saw.* Document D2 is the September 2026 statement, but the notes give figures at 31 Oct. I raised G1 and settled nothing. The block covers this through its rule on figures, periods or dates that do not agree with a listed document; that rule is new and this is its first use.
3. *Item 6 time.* The motion record has no time for the suggestion, and the notes say "about 19:12". I kept the notes' wording, "at about 19:12".
4. *The Secretary in the closed-session line.* The notes say "I" and "Pell and I". I wrote "the Secretary", the title in the meeting details, for the person who wrote the notes.

**Differences from the earlier published example output.** That output left out "Documents: none." and "No motion recorded." on items 1, 6, 7 and 8, named the abstainer in the Votes line, numbered gaps out of draft order, dropped the role "Director", showed times for people present throughout, printed "Carried." with a full stop, restated "10 Nov" as "10 November 2026" and "the October statement" as "the October financial statement", and wrote "No notes were taken" for the notes' "No notes". The example has been rebuilt from this run and the two now match word for word.

**Any fact in this output that is not in the inputs:** none that I found. I checked each name, time and figure against the inputs.

**Reviewer checklist read against this output.** Items 1 to 5 hold: motion text is as supplied, tallies and results match the record with no result invented, no quorum statement is written, every action has its owner and date from the notes or says it has none, and the decision without a motion is marked as noted. Items 6 and 7 are for the secretary and chair: they must settle the five gaps and put the draft into the board's house style, which I could not do.

**Limits of this trial.** It is one run on one invented meeting, made by the session that revised the template, in which no stop condition was triggered. The acceptance tests that need a stop (header mismatch, asked about quorum, asked to tidy a motion, tally mismatch) were not run, and neither were the new rules for a blank motion time or a person not on the roster. This is not a test in a client environment.

## Clarifications made after review

After an independent review, small clarifications were made to the template and nothing in this record above was changed. The instruction block now says: never put another time, such as the time people returned or the adjournment time, in place of a missing closed-session time (the point raised under "Where I still had to choose", item 1); a closed-session gap is also raised when there are no notes; a recused person whom the notes say left the room for that vote needs no gap for that alone; a minutes style and an optional sample of earlier minutes may be supplied; and a gap reference ends the line it concerns. These match what this run already produced on the example, and I did not re-run the template after the clarifications; I read the example output against them and found it consistent. The review also noted that this run was made by the session that revised the template and not by a blind reader. A run by a fresh reader has not been done and would be a stronger trial. The stop conditions, a blank motion time and a person not on the roster remain unexercised.
